The beeswax comes in different shades of yellow due to the types of flowers and plants the Carniolan (Apis mellifera carnica) honeybee and the Italian (Apis mellifera ligustica) honeybee forage on. The variety of pollen and nectar sources each bee collects naturally influences the color of the wax they produce — making each batch beautifully unique!

The light white residue is from the mold release spray to help remove the beeswax candle from the candle mold.

Some candle molds are defective due to the manufacture's creating the candle mold or during shipping.
